# Amazon Ads and Walmart Connect expansion feasibility

**Public desk research; checked 2026-11-09.** US marketplace focus. No registrations, logins, outreach, purchases, or account-level verification. “Documented” means an official public source describes the capability; it does not establish that a particular advertiser has permission or that an export succeeds. Some help pages were readable only through the search index; JavaScript-only pages and unresolved differences are identified below. Every linked external source in this memo was checked on **2026-11-09**, including links in the final tables and CSV instructions.

**Decision:** both channels are **feasible only with added controls or format restrictions**, plus ingestion work and new validation. Neither merits “current engine + adapter” as an incrementality product for an ordinary mixed retail-media account. Amazon sellers have a comparatively clear manual spend/total-sales path. Walmart Marketplace sellers have documented non-attributed sales data too, but a 62-day search-spend retrieval notice creates a serious cold-start history constraint. Walmart 1P omnichannel outcomes require a separate supplier-data entitlement; without an appropriate total-sales export, that segment is **blocked on data access**.

The required outcome changes from Shopify revenue to the advertiser's total Amazon or Walmart retail sales for a fixed, matched entity. Platform-attributed sales remain a comparison only. A platform's column named “total sales” can still mean **total attributed sales**, including halo purchases; that is never the regression outcome. Do not combine retailer outcomes into one series or assume Amazon/Walmart media affect only Shopify revenue.

### Total outcomes: Seller Central and Vendor Central

**Seller Central manual route:** Reports → Business Reports → **By date: Sales and Traffic**, daily. Download `.csv`; request `Date` and **Ordered Product Sales**, all fulfillment channels, the complete seller catalog and marketplace, with B2B and B2C included. The official seller guide documents daily/weekly/monthly reports, download, two years of history and that unlabeled totals include both customer types. Do not add a B2B subset to its inclusive total. These are retail business reports, not ad-conversion reports. [Amazon seller business-report guide](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-business-reports) (checked 2026-11-09). Exact capitalization of the delivered header must be read from the file.

**SP-API seller route:** **Sales and Traffic Business Report**, `GET_SALES_AND_TRAFFIC_REPORT`, JSON, `dateGranularity=DAY`; use `salesAndTrafficByDate[].date` and `salesByDate.orderedProductSales` (amount/currency). Entire-catalog totals are separate from ASIN summaries; do not add them together. Lookback: two years. Seller availability; **Brand Analytics SP-API role** required. This report's seller availability must not be confused with Brand Registry requirements for other brand reports. The schema confirms by-date totals and only one marketplace per report; ASIN summaries must not be assumed to be a date × ASIN panel. [Official seller report schema](https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/schemas/reports/sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json) (checked 2026-11-09).

**Vendor route:** **Vendor Sales Report**, `GET_VENDOR_SALES_REPORT`; use `reportPeriod=DAY`. Required `distributorView` and `sellingProgram` define coverage. Daily lookback **1,460 days**, request span **15 days**; non-Saturday daily data SLA 72 hours. Vendors need the Brand Analytics role. Vendor Central's exact UI download path/headers are **UNVERIFIED**. [SP-API analytics report definitions](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-analytics) (checked 2026-11-09).

The official schema has `salesAggregate[]`, `salesByAsin[]`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `orderedRevenue`, `orderedUnits`. **Ordered revenue/units are populated only for `distributorView=MANUFACTURING`**; ordered revenue is adjusted for returns. `SOURCING` cannot be presumed to supply this outcome. Extract dated daily aggregate records, not a multi-day sum. Manufacturing-view entitlement and catalog alignment must be verified; otherwise the requested ordered-sales design is blocked for that vendor. [Official vendor report schema](https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/schemas/reports/vendorSalesReport.json) (checked 2026-11-09).

**Practical scope:** the seller total is that merchant's sales, not every seller's sales of its brand. Vendor manufacturing coverage can differ from sourcing coverage. Match ads, catalog, country, currency and commercial entity before fitting. Vendor retail ordered revenue is consumer sell-through, not the vendor's wholesale shipments or purchase orders. Use one fixed outcome definition; refunds, cancellations, taxes, shipping and revisions require a documented reconciliation rather than silently treating ordered revenue as Shopify net revenue. Ads API approval does not grant SP-API roles or Vendor Central access.

### Identification mapping — analytical judgments, not platform claims

Risk is relative to **same-day OLS + DOW + trend + HC3**, not to whether a format can work. “Lower” never establishes exogeneity. Attribution windows show that the platform measures later purchases; they do not estimate causal carryover.

| Amazon format | Endogeneity risk | Carryover risk | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsored Products, brand/defensive and automatic retail targeting | **High** | **Medium** | Search/click opportunities increase with shopper demand; brand demand drives both sales and realized spend. Dynamic conversion-based bidding compounds this. Some purchases occur later. This maps directly to catastrophic v2 S4. |
| Sponsored Products, generic/nonbrand | **High** (potentially less severe than brand) | **Medium** | Category demand, promotions, stock and competitive auctions still drive clicks/spend; a nonbrand label or fixed bid does not make spend independent. |
| Sponsored Brands, keyword/search and video | **High** | **High** | Keyword opportunities follow demand; brand-discovery/video exposure can influence future searches and purchases. A mixed goal campaign blends harvesting and demand creation. |
| Sponsored Display, contextual/remarketing | **High** for remarketing; **Medium** for contextual prospecting | **High** | Recent shoppers and visitors drive eligible audience size and sales propensity. Broad prospecting is less directly tied to same-day searches but still optimized; repeated exposures create lag. |
| DSP display/video/audio/streaming | **Medium**, **High** for retargeting or conversion optimization; **lower only conditionally** for independently scheduled broad reach | **High** | Auction optimization and audience demand can drive delivery; scheduled reach can reduce that link. Video/audio effects and cross-format search response can extend beyond the day. |

**Mechanics verified:** Amazon documents real-time Sponsored Products dynamic bidding based on conversion likelihood, and scheduled bid increases by hour/day/date range. Official pages disagree on the maximum up-adjustment for non-top-search placements (current dedicated guide says up to 100% for all placements, an FBA guide says 50% elsewhere); the exact cap is **UNVERIFIED**, while demand-responsive bidding is established. [Dedicated dynamic-bidding guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/dynamic-bidding-sponsored-products), [conflicting FBA guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/fba), [schedule-based rules](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-gb/resources/whats-new/schedule-based-bid-rules-available-for-sponsored-products) (checked 2026-11-09).

Brands theme targeting continuously optimizes keywords with shopping trends; Display offers views/purchases remarketing and bid optimization. DSP Performance+ predicts action probability in real time and adjusts bids. [Brands goal-based campaigns](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/resources/whats-new/sponsored-brands-goal-based-campaigns), [Display guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/guides/display-ads-guide), [DSP Performance+ guide](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-ca/library/guides/dsp-performance-plus) (checked 2026-11-09). **Inference:** these mechanisms can link spend to latent demand. They do not prove the magnitude or sign of bias in a particular account. A fixed daily budget is a ceiling/target, not a randomized realized-spend instrument; dayparting set in advance is not itself proof of same-day feedback.

### Amazon verdict and missing columns

**Feasible only with added controls or format restrictions.** The panel can contain retailer-local `date`, campaign identifiers, daily `spend`, a seller's daily total `ordered_product_sales` (or vendor `ordered_revenue`), and platform `attributed_sales`/orders for comparison. DOW/trend are derived. IDs must be supplied or mapped where the manual file provides names only. API approval is avoidable for a manual pilot; exact Brands/Display/DSP API schemas remain unverified.

What is missing from the minimal panel is identification: independent demand shocks, complete daily price/promotion/inventory/Featured Offer histories, bidding-rule changes, other traffic/media and delayed effects. Some business-report traffic/offer metrics exist, but they are not automatically valid causal controls: contemporaneous sessions and branded searches can themselves be caused by ads. A useful expansion would require a preregistered control/design strategy, or genuinely independently varied, short-lag media with other active campaigns accounted for. **Simply deleting search spend while leaving search campaigns running is not a valid format restriction.**

### Total outcomes: Marketplace versus 1P supplier

**Marketplace manual route:** Seller Center → Sales Insights → **Account Sales Report** or **Item Sales Report** → Download CSV. Account report summarizes the seller's Walmart.com sales; Item report is SKU-level. Metrics include **GMV, Units Sold, Orders and AUR**; default range 30 days, customizable, current-day data may be partial. Official help confirms CSV downloads but does **not specify whether the downloaded account CSV contains daily rows or only range totals**, or the maximum daily lookback: **UNVERIFIED**. A single range total cannot be spread over days. [Sales Insights overview](https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/account-and-item-sales-overview) (checked 2026-11-09).

**Marketplace documented daily API/export route:** **Item Performance report**, `reportType=ITEM_PERFORMANCE`, **`reportVersion=v3`**, `dateGranularity=DAILY`, CSV. Fields include `Data Start Time`, `Data End Time`, `Item ID`, `SKU ID`, `GMV`, `Authorized Sales`, `Cancelled Sales`, `Refunded Sales`, `legacySlrId`. Sum nonduplicated item/SKU rows over the complete seller catalog by day to obtain **total seller GMV**, not attributed revenue. [US Item Performance request, versions and fields](https://developer.walmart.com/us-marketplace/docs/request-an-item-performance-report) (checked 2026-11-09).

This is **Marketplace API access**, distinct from WCPN Ads API approval: Walmart's developer guide allows an existing seller to obtain its own client ID/secret, while solution providers need delegated access. It still requires credentials and technical setup by the seller outside this research; an ordinary seller need not become an Ads API partner to access its own sales API. **Actual v3 availability and daily coverage in an SMB account remain untested.** [Marketplace developer onboarding](https://developer.walmart.com/global-marketplace/page/getting-started) (checked 2026-11-09).

### Identification mapping — analytical judgments

| Walmart format | Endogeneity risk | Carryover risk | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsored Products / Sponsored Search, including branded keywords and automatic targeting | **High** (brand is the catastrophic case) | **Medium**; **High** for brand/video storytelling variants | Shopper searches and click volume follow current demand; bids and realized spend change with conversion opportunity. Nonbrand/category search remains demand-responsive. Video can create later demand. |
| Onsite display, item/contextual and retargeting placements | **Medium** for broad reach; **High** for retargeting | **High** | Site traffic and recent shopping behavior affect delivery; auction pacing adds feedback. Broad inventory reduces direct query dependence but does not guarantee independent spend. |
| Offsite display / DSP / social / CTV | **Medium** for broad prospecting; **High** for retailer-audience retargeting/conversion optimization; conditionally **lower** for independently scheduled reach | **High** | Delivery optimization and retail-derived audience selection connect spend to purchasing propensity; later retail purchases and other media pathways are likely omitted by same-day OLS. Marketplace-only outcomes can additionally omit in-store effects. |

**Mechanics verified:** Walmart's current Sponsored Products setup guide recommends **Dynamic Bidding** for real-time bid changes and says low budgets can cap out before peak hours. This establishes the feature but not its full prediction inputs. A Walmart Connect case study explicitly describes partner autopacing and dayparting to capture peak conversion/traffic times—**partner capability, not proof every SMB has a native dayparting switch**. Display's own documentation describes a dynamic bidding engine adjusting bids with campaign pacing. A universal native feature specifically called “retail-aware pacing” is **UNVERIFIED**; do not invent it. [Sponsored Products setup](https://www.walmartconnect.com/insights/easy-campaign-set-up), [partner dayparting case](https://www.walmartconnect.com/resources/case-studies/2024/laundry-detergent-pacvue-sponsored-videos-boost-visibility), [Display auction mechanics](https://www.walmartconnect.com/resources/articles/2024/giving-advertisers-greater-pricing-control) (checked 2026-11-09).

**Inference:** even fixed bids leave spend dependent on search volume, relevance, availability, auction competition and clicks. Choosing budget increases around sales events can correlate spend with baseline sales. Pacing and prescheduled dayparting are not automatically endogenous; the problem is dependence on omitted demand or performance feedback. HC3 addresses neither omitted demand nor omitted adstock.

### Walmart verdict and missing columns

**Marketplace: feasible only with added controls or format restrictions, conditional on actual export acceptance.** In documented APIs, the columns exist: `date` + `campaignId` + `adSpend` from aggregated search ad groups; `date` + `campaignExtId` + `adSpend` for Display; item-day total `GMV` summed to seller-day outcome; attributed sales/orders separately. DOW and trend are derived. Campaign names/formats come from account metadata; ID namespace must include search/display and advertiser. The sales API does not unlock the Ads API.

**1P: the same modeling restriction, and blocked for any supplier unable to supply a matched daily total-sales series.** Charter's daily Omni Sales route is documented; equivalent free-Basic CSV access is not verified. Search's short history and partner permissions are additional onboarding constraints. Manual US daily spend and sales CSV layouts need verification; therefore do not promise that every SMB can immediately supply the panel without a partner or technical extraction.

Missing from either minimal panel: sufficient demand controls and independent variation, delayed response, complete retail operations history, and possibly offsite spend/in-store outcomes. A seller-only study can be validly scoped to seller sales, but cannot claim the brand's omnichannel return. A 62-day history is a limitation to test and disclose, not permission to label a short panel equivalent to the 90-day v2 program.

### 2. Exact CSV sets to request

These are **our proposed normalized ingestion schemas**, not invented platform report names or a claim that the current app already accepts them. The seller may send native exports; the adapter produces the three canonical CSVs, preserving raw originals and provenance. A “three CSV” package may require several native downloads or API chunks. Require a common calendar of preferably **90 or more complete days**, one fixed entity/catalog/country/currency and explicit time zones. Do not silently zero-fill absent outcome dates; verified no-sales days are explicit zeros. No customer PII is needed.

**Amazon seller — exactly three core CSVs:**

1. **`amazon_campaign_daily_spend.csv`** — columns `date,advertiser_id,marketplace,currency,campaign_id,campaign_name,ad_product,spend`. Source: daily Campaign/unified reporting across **every** active Amazon format; approved API SP export acceptable. Preserve native Cost/Spend and fee definition in provenance; resolve duplicate/reused names through a stable ID map. Add seller-declared `targeting_class,bid_strategy` columns when available; unknown stays unknown. Include all campaigns influencing this sales entity, including relevant externally bought media exports merged under distinct platform IDs.
2. **`amazon_total_sales_daily.csv`** — `date,seller_id,marketplace,currency,total_ordered_product_sales`. Source: Seller Central **By date: Sales and Traffic**, inclusive total, entire fixed seller catalog, all fulfillment channels; SP-API by-date total is an alternative. Use one row per day. A vendor substitutes `vendor_id,total_ordered_revenue` from Vendor Sales Report **manufacturing view**, records selling program, and reconciles its return-adjusted definition. Do not aggregate an ASIN range-total report into fictional daily rows.
3. **`amazon_campaign_daily_attribution.csv`** — `date,advertiser_id,campaign_id,ad_product,attribution_window_days,attribution_basis,attributed_sales,attributed_orders,currency`. Source: the **same daily ad reports**, separating Cost into file 1 and attributed Sales/Purchases into file 3. Record click/view basis and traffic/conversion date convention; different windows remain separate rows, never added together. If orders are unavailable, leave null and document; missing comparison does not permit replacing total sales. Refresh after the relevant attribution window and retain extraction timestamps.

**Walmart Marketplace seller — exactly three core CSVs, conditional on actual access:**

1. **`walmart_campaign_daily_spend.csv`** — `date,advertiser_id,campaign_id,campaign_name,ad_product,currency,spend`. Source: US daily Ad Center exports **only after their daily grain is verified**, or WCPN partner Snapshot v2 **`adGroup`** CSVs using `date,campaignId,adGroupId,adSpend`, summed across mutually exclusive groups. Display partner **Campaign Report** supplies `date,campaignExtId,campaignName,adSpend`. Preserve namespace/advertiser; add offsite buying-platform spend if absent from Connect. Require saved history beyond the conservative 62-day retrieval horizon, or collect prospectively. Do not promise a 90-day immediate backfill.
2. **`walmart_total_sales_daily.csv`** — `date,seller_id,marketplace,currency,total_gmv`. Source: daily **Item Performance v3** CSVs, `Data Start Time,Data End Time,Item ID,SKU ID,GMV,legacySlrId`, disjoint ≤15-day requests and one nonduplicated full-catalog sum per day. Use a declared common window if available, keep online/store/total variants distinct, and never sum total with its constituent channels. Obtain actual report timezone and click/view basis rather than assuming parity across search/display.

**Walmart 1P substitute for file 2:** `walmart_supplier_total_sales_daily.csv` with `date,supplier_id,geo_region,operating_company,currency,sales_scope,total_retail_sales`; derive from authorized **Omni Sales** using `bus_dt,wm_item_nbr,catlg_item_id,svc_chnl_nm,order_chnl_nm,geo_region_cd,op_cmpny_cd,auth_based_gmv_amt`. An equivalent Report Builder export is acceptable only after its dated fields, full coverage and measure are verified. **No such file → no 1P fit.**

**Required accompanying metadata, inside the CSVs or a plain-text manifest:** source report/version; extraction time; reporting timezone and DST convention; IDs; fixed catalog and sales-channel scope; currency; definition of spend and outcome; attribution basis/windows; zero-versus-missing semantics; any missing formats; bid/rule changes and known flight/pause dates. ### 3. Validation to rerun before any channel claim

Use the local [v2 preregistration](../validation/v2-preregistration.md) and [v2 calibration results](../validation/v2-calibration-2026-11-09.md), both inspected on 2026-11-09. Existing outcomes were S1 FAIL, S2 PASS, S3 PASS under its limited stress gates, S4 FAIL, S5 FAIL, S6 PASS. **S3 PASS did not mean nominal coverage.** S4's zero-effect demand-driven brand campaign was labeled positive 99.95% of the time, with 0.05% coverage; S5's long-run 4x campaign averaged approximately 1.85x downward bias. These results are reasons to withhold channel-wide causal claims, not to tune pass thresholds.

1. **Freeze each adapter and engine, preregister before running.** Preserve the original six-scenario replication, 4,000 independent datasets per scenario, 90-day horizon, seed scheme, truth isolation, HC3-t and the joint-campaign fit. Run separate Amazon and Walmart versions with realistic observed spend scales, campaign counts, outcome distributions and date conventions, recording every departure as a new specification. No silent reuse of Shopify calibration as retail validation.
2. **Mirror S1/S2 baseline and realistic order-value noise.** Use each retailer's declared ordered-sales/GMV definition; reconcile tax/shipping/discounts/refunds/cancellations rather than adding random AOV to a monthly total. Verify adapter output matches an in-memory sufficient-statistic panel and source totals. Include small-seller volumes, intermittent campaigns and many correlated spend columns.
3. **Mirror S3 flights plus serial demand shocks.** Test budget cap-outs, pauses and multi-day flights, common retail events, co-movement across formats, outcome revision delays and residual serial dependence. Amazon: Prime/deal events and Featured Offer/inventory changes; Walmart: event pricing, availability and fulfillment/channel changes. HC3 is not serial-correlation robust. Repeat paired HC3 versus the preregistered HAC4/HAC7 comparisons without assuming HAC fixes bias; any engine change needs its own preregistration.
4. **Mirror S4 endogeneity, per channel and format.** Amazon: brand and generic Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, automatic/dynamic bidding, remarketing, demand-linked bid/budget updates. Walmart: branded/generic search, automatic targeting, dynamic/target-performance strategies if present, and partner pacing/dayparting. Generate zero-causal-effect demand-harvesting campaigns; quantify false positives and contamination of other jointly fitted coefficients. A fixed-bid variant must still allow search traffic to drive clicks/spend. Test proposed controls or restrictions against this failure before accepting them.
5. **Mirror S5 omitted carryover.** Begin with normalized geometric adstock λ=0.5 as frozen, then preregister channel/format lag sensitivities, cross-format search effects and post-window tails. Amazon: Display, Brands video and DSP; Walmart: onsite Display, offsite/social/CTV. Evaluate against cumulative long-run revenue truth, not the misspecified same-day target. Longer attribution windows are not an adstock correction.
6. **Mirror S6 signed/boundary effects and decision labels.** Preserve practical-zero region ±0.2x, equivalence precedence, positive/negative/inconclusive/unavailable, signed estimates and low-power reporting. Do not call a wide interval “no incrementality” or convert retail revenue-iROAS into profit advice without a supplied margin. Compare only scope/window-compatible platform attribution columns.
7. **Keep v2 metrics and frozen gates explicit.** Report signed bias, RMSE, mean SE, |bias|/mean SE, empirical 95% coverage with exact Clopper–Pearson lower bound, width, label/confusion rates, false flags, unavailable rate and MDE80 distributions. Original gates: S1/S2 CP lower ≥0.92; |bias| ≤0.1 mean SE in every scenario; false flags ≤10% for truth ≥0.25x; zero-truth positive rate ≤10% in S1/S2. Stress coverage stays visible. Preregister any stronger requirements for a restricted-channel causal claim before results; do not relabel an S4/S5 failure as successful expansion.
8. **Channel-specific ingestion acceptance and short-history tests.** Verify calendar completeness across sources, timezone alignment (including vendor PST), currencies, stable IDs, archived campaigns, zero-spend days, absent outcome days, request-boundary duplication, item/feed revisions, and no double-counting ad-group/placement or total/channel subtotals. Amazon: reproduce one panel from manual versus API sources where available and test old/new report migrations. Walmart: explicitly test 62-day versus 90-day power/calibration, v3 15-day sales chunks, missing ad history, partner versus UI exports, supplier delta application and online-versus-store scope. A failed data-access or completeness check returns **unavailable**, not an estimate from attributed sales.
9. **Benchmark a selected eligible cohort against independent experiments before external causal claims.** Preregister a randomized pause/budget assignment or suitable holdout with adequate lag follow-up and scope-matched total retailer outcomes. A platform lift report can be a comparator only after understanding its design, eligibility, estimand and uncertainty. This memo authorizes no live campaign changes. Desk research establishes possible data paths; it does not establish identification or field validation.
